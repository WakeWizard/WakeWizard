02 / FIRST BOOT

A few steps to join your network.

After installation, keep your ESP32 powered on. Continue setup over Wi-Fi.

  1. Connect to WakeWizard-XXXX

    Find the Wi-Fi network created by your board.
    Password: wakewizard-setup

  2. Open setup

    Visit 192.168.4.1 while connected to the WakeWizard network. Choose your language, hostname and Wi-Fi network, then create an administrator password of at least 8 characters.

  3. Wake your devices

    After the board restarts, reconnect to your network and open its IP address or your chosen hostname followed by .local. Add the devices you want to wake.

Need a hand?

My board does not appear or connect

Use a USB data cable, try another USB port and close PlatformIO, Arduino IDE or any other application using the serial port. If the connection stalls, hold the BOOT button on your board while retrying, then release it when writing starts. Some boards require the manufacturer’s USB driver.

Can I use Safari or my phone?

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer with Web Serial support for this installer. The message above reflects your browser’s available features. After installation, you can also access the WakeWizard interface from your phone.

I already have WakeWizard installed

This installer performs a complete reinstall and erases your data. For updates, use the OTA section in your device’s interface and consult the user guide. Always keep a backup before reinstalling.
